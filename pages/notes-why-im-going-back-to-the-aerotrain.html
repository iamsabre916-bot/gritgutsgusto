Crisis hits your project. Now what?

5 November 2026 · Ir. Ts. Dr. Bibi Sabrena Sakandar Khan

Every project hits a crisis sooner or later. The schedule slips, something fails right before handover, and suddenly everyone is looking at you for the next move.

Nobody really teaches you what to do in that moment. Most of us learn it the hard way, in the middle of one. I did, over 20 years of airport and infrastructure projects. Some days went to plan. Many didn't.

So I'm hosting my first santai session, to share the honest version of how to manage a crisis and manoeuvre a project back on track.

A santai morning, one long table

On Saturday 21 November 2026, I'm sitting down with a small group of young executives and emerging leaders. One long table, nasi lemak and teh tarik, and real stories from projects that didn't go to plan.

We'll talk about three things:

  • Grit: pushing through the setbacks, once you've weighed the risks.
  • Guts: the hard calls, and how to make them under pressure. Bring a real dilemma from your own work and we'll work through it together.
  • Gusto: keeping a tired team going, and why every milestone deserves a celebration.

If you're a young engineer, a new supervisor, or someone who has just been handed your first team or first project, this morning is for you.
